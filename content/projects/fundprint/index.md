##### Overview

Fundprint is a nonpartisan browser extension. It underlines the names of US federal candidates and members of Congress on the pages you browse; hovering over a name shows how that candidate's campaign is funded, from official FEC filings via the OpenFEC API. Clicking through opens a full breakdown of the candidate's funding, or of a specific funder such as a PAC and who funds it.

It presents facts only, with no labels, scores, or ratings. Page text is matched entirely on your device, and individual donors are grouped by employer, never shown by name.

<small>MVP feature-complete; not yet published to the Chrome Web Store.</small>
