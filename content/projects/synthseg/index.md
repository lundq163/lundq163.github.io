##### Overview

CDNI's fork of [SynthSeg](https://github.com/BBillot/SynthSeg), the contrast-agnostic MRI segmentation method by Benjamin Billot and colleagues. My contributions adapt it for the center's lifespan segmentation work: adding lifespan label files, generating priors for whatever ages appear in the input filenames, supporting T2-only inputs, and adding a notebook for visualizing results.
